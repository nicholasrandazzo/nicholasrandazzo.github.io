Introduction

How I got here.

I studied biology and philosophy at the University of Florida before beginning medical school in New York. Along the way, I’ve worked in chemical biology, ocular oncology, philosophy, and outdoor media.

Those experiences have made me attentive to both sides of medicine: the technical work of understanding and treating disease, and the judgment required to care for a person well.
